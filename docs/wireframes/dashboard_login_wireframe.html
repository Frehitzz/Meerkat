Design & UX Decisions

  • Minimalist Monochrome Theme: Clean black and white core design with neutral dark tones and standard functional accent tags.
  • Single OAuth Flow: Login with Facebook for Business covers both FB Page & connected IG Business account.
  • Platform Tags: High-visibility badges differentiate Facebook vs Instagram inquiries instantly.
  • Read-Only Banner: Clear warning during Phase 6 that replies must be sent via Meta Business Suite until Phase 10+.
  • Widget Placeholders: Prepared UI grid slots for Phase 8 Sales Analytics & Phase 9 AI Strategy.
